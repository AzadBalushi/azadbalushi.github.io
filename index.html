About

Executive Biography

A career dedicated to bridging business, communication, and technology.

With more than two decades of experience at the intersection of business, communication, and technology, Azad Abdul Hameed Balushi has established himself as a trusted architect of digital transformation. His career spans enterprise-scale collaboration platforms, employee experience programs, and communication strategies that have reshaped how large organizations operate and connect.

At SABIC, one of the world's largest petrochemical companies, Azad played a pivotal role in advancing digital transformation initiatives — leading SharePoint and Microsoft 365 platform strategies, designing digital workplace solutions, and driving adoption across more than 16 departments and 1,200 employees. His work bridges the gap between technical capability and human experience, ensuring technology investments translate into measurable business value.

Azad's expertise encompasses enterprise communications, knowledge management, Power Platform solutions, UI/UX strategy, web technologies, and multimedia production. He is known for his ability to engage executive stakeholders, align cross-functional teams, and lead change adoption programs that embed new ways of working into organizational culture. Today, he continues to explore how generative AI and modern collaboration tools can unlock the next generation of employee experience.
